# Cocares page: photo selection

Working document. Every photo in `Cocars/Photos/` was viewed individually. Paths are relative to `Cocars/Photos/`. The pool is 57 files, not 58. `atelier-01.jpg` lives in `materials/`, not in an `atelier/` folder as `selecao.md` suggests.

Pixel sizes matter for crops: most files are 1368×1824 (3:4) or 1080×1920 (9:16). Crop coordinates below are in original pixels and approximate.

## Two threads worth keeping

- **The teal piece runs through the page.** The hero (`teal-raffia-finished-01`) and the second choice for the making section (`teal-raffia-process-11`) are the same piece: the reader meets it finished, then sees its shells and aquamarine beads being threaded.
- **The red-black piece carries the "made for one person" arc.** Fabi's hand holds the gold trident charm in movement (b); the same trident hangs from the finished piece on the wall in movement (d).

Both threads are worth preserving if first choices get swapped.

1. **`teal-raffia/teal-raffia-finished-01.jpg`** — first choice. ## 5. Processing notes

### Tonal fixes

- **`teal-raffia-finished-01`** (hero, #3): underexposed by about +0.5 to +0.7 EV. The wall is grey-green; warm it slightly toward the site's cream. This is the most important fix on the page.
- **`blue-orange-finished-01`**: the wall is slightly cool and grey. Apply a mild warm white balance.
- **`white-gold-detail-01`** and **`white-silver-detail-01`**: dim and grey, shot on bedding at night. Lift exposure and shadows, and neutralise the grey cast. Expect noise. Do not push the whites to clip, since the texture is the subject.
- **`rainbow-feathers-finished-01`**: the purple light cast can be reduced but not removed, because the wall and the white cord are lavender. Correct gently, because the feather colours shift with it.
- **`red-black-detail-01`**: slight cool cast on the white bedding. Neutralise.
- **`green-rasta-feather-finished-01`**: the phone over-sharpened it. Add a touch of softening if halos show at display size.
- **`jute-cowrie-raffia-finished-01`**, **`purple-raffia-finished-01`**, **`red-black-finished-01`**, **`orange-rust-finished-01`**, **`green-black-finished-01`** and **`green-black-finished-05`**: fine as they are. At most, match the wall tone across the set so the carousel reads as one series.

### Cloning (flag only)

- **`rainbow/rainbow-finished-01.jpg`**: the piece hangs above what looks like Fabi's altar or work table, with incense, crystals and cupping glasses. That would be a beautiful image for (d) with the table kept, but a slow cooker (right), a clock (left) and a pink sponge would need cloning out. A crop can only remove the whole table.
- **`red-black-process-06`**: after the 1:1 crop, a little patterned fabric remains in the bottom-right corner. Leave it or blur it. Cloning is not needed.
